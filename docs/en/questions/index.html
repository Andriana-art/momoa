Have a question?

  • Is MOMOA a real gallery?

    MOMOA is an imagined gallery created for a university project. You can explore the works and their stories, just as you would in a real collection.

  • Are all the sculptures Greek?

    The collection reaches beyond ancient Greece, with Roman copies and later works inspired by Greek myths. Each artwork page helps you place the work in its time.

  • How do I sign up?

    Simply head to our Sign Up page and enter your name and email.

    Note: As this is an academic project, the form is for demonstration purposes only and no data is stored.
